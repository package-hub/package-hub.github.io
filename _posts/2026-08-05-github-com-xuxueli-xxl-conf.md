## [xxl-conf](https://github.com/xuxueli/xxl-conf)

### A configuration center and service registry.（分布式配置中心与注册中心，XXL-CONF）

XXL-CONF is a distributed configuration center and registration center. providing dynamic configuration management, service registration and discovery and other core capabilities. with "lightweight, second-level real-time push, multi-environment, cross-language, cross-room, permission control" and other features. Now open source, out of the box.

XXL-CONF 是一个分布式配置中心与注册中心，提供 动态配置管理、服务注册与发现 等核心能力；拥有 “轻量级、秒级实时推送、多环境、跨语言、跨机房、权限控制” 等特性。现已开放源代码，开箱即用。
